FAQ

Good
questions.

Not answered here? Email hello@scintova.com and you'll get a straight answer the same day.

Is this actually made with AI?

Yes, and we're upfront about it. We work from your photos and your real packaging, and anyone on screen is a real person who has agreed to appear in ads. The AI replaces the crew, the studio and the waiting.

Won't it look fake?

Watch the ads above and judge for yourself. A wrong label, an odd hand or a face that's off gets remade, never hidden. If we can't get your product looking right, we'll tell you before you've paid anything.

Do I need to post you the product?

No, good photos are enough. If it's something unusual, we might ask you to send one over.

How quickly will I get it?

A spec ad is usually back within a few days. A test pack takes about a week, and most of that is waiting for you to approve the scripts.

What if I don't like it?

Then you don't pay. The spec ad costs nothing unless you use it, and every paid order includes changes.

Can I run the ads on Meta, TikTok and YouTube?

Yes. Every paid order includes the right to run the ads as paid ads for 90 days, on any platform.

Can I keep an ad running for longer?

Yes. Past 90 days it's 30% more. Just email and say which ad.

Who will I be dealing with?

Bohan Sun, in London. He reads every email and makes the ads himself. No account managers and no sales team.
