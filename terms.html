Last updated 24 November 2026

These are the terms for ads made by Scintova, an AI video ad studio in London run by Bohan Sun. Questions go to hello@scintova.com.

Spec ads

We can make one ad for your product before you've paid anything. If you don't want to use it, you don't pay for it. If you do, it costs £100 and comes cleared for 90 days of paid use.

Prices

  • Your spec ad: £100
  • Test pack, three ads with three angles: £249
  • Monthly, ten ads a month: £690 a month
  • Hero film, widescreen and vertical: from £450

The price for your order is the one we agree by email before any work starts.

Payment

Half upfront, and the other half when you're happy with the finished ads.

Scripts and changes

You see the idea and every line of the script before anything is made, and you can change it or ask for a new one. Once the ad is made, a test pack includes one round of changes and a hero film includes two.

Running the ads

Every paid ad comes with the right to run it as a paid ad, on any platform, for 90 days from when we deliver the final files. To keep running an ad after that, it costs 30 percent of its price.

What you send us

Please only send photos, logos and product claims you're allowed to use, and tell us anything you can't claim about your product. You check and approve the script, including any claims in it, before the ad is made.

People in our ads

Anyone who appears in a Scintova ad is a real person who has agreed to appear in ads.

Our showreel

The ads on our home page are spec work. We made them to show what we can do, and the brands shown didn't commission them.
